Who Is Cleon Williams?

Cleon Williams is a Jamaican User Experience Designer, System Architect, and Creative Director focused on building lean, local-first software and high-impact digital media for the Global South. With over nine years of experience as an Industry Coach in animation, game development, and voice acting, he bridges the gap between human-centered design, resilient engineering, and creative education whilst in pursuit of a B.Ed. in Computer Science.
